ACCEPTED groups in `data/paired_perturbation_production/`. No weights were
recalibrated, no consensus/flux scores were combined, TAU (1e-6) was not
changed, and no simulation was rerun. This is a numerical/statistical summary
only — no biological interpretation is asserted.

## 7. Production vs. pilot comparison (where applicable)

(`07a_pilot_vs_production_correlation.csv`, `07b_pilot_vs_production_merged_values.csv`)

Pilot data exists for only 2 of the 10 groups (44-reaction curated subset,
`M_effective` = 120·n_seeds vs. production's full-network M=800):

| group | pilot M_eff | n overlapping reactions | Pearson r | Pearson p | Spearman r | Spearman p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSE126848/obese_no_NAFLD | 240 (2 seeds) | 44 | 0.99957 | 6.2e-66 | 0.4052 | 0.0064 |
| GSE135251/healthy | 480 (4 seeds) | 40 | 0.98198 | 4.2e-29 | 0.5062 | 0.00086 |

Pearson correlation (magnitude-driven) is very high in both groups; Spearman
correlation (rank-driven) is moderate (0.41 and 0.51) — the large gap between
the two is a direct numeric consequence of most reactions clustering near the
zero/noise floor (Section 1), where relative rank order is unstable even
though the few large-magnitude reactions dominate and agree closely between
pilot and production on absolute scale. The other 8 groups have no pilot
counterpart and are not compared.
